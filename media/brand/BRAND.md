# Mayuri's Yarn Tales — Brand Guidelines

The mark is a crochet hook through a ball of yarn: what Mayuri makes, by hand.
Three flat colours, no gradients. Wordmark is Caveat (handwritten), turned into
outlines so the SVGs look right without the font installed.

## Typography

- Logo and handwritten touches: **Caveat**, bold
- Site text: the system font (SF Pro on Apple, Inter elsewhere)

## Usage Rules

- Minimum icon size: 16×16px
- Always maintain original aspect ratio
- Do not stretch, rotate, skew, or add drop shadows
- Use monochrome variant for single-color printing
- Minimum clear space: 25% of icon width on all sides
