Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Mayuri's Yarn Tales ("Mayuri'z", "we") is a small handmade crochet shop in India run by Mayuri. This page explains what we collect when you use mayurisyarntales.online or our Instagram and Facebook pages, and what we do with it. We keep it simple: we only use your details to answer you and make your order.

What we collect

  • Order and enquiry form: your name, phone number, email, the piece or category you're asking about, and the details you write. These are sent to our shop email so we can reply.
  • "Email me new arrivals": your email address, sent to our shop inbox through Web3Forms, so we can tell you about new pieces.
  • Visit counter: a simple anonymous count of visits (no names, no tracking across sites).
  • Your browser: remembers whether you've already seen the welcome popup and been counted, so we don't show or count it again. This stays on your device.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not sell or rent your details, and we do not share them with anyone except as needed to deliver your order (for example, giving your address to the courier).

Instagram and Facebook

We post photos of our own work to our Instagram account (@mayurisyarntales) and Facebook Page (Mayuri's Yarn Tales), partly with a small app we made for our own pages ("MayuriZ"). That app only publishes our posts. It does not collect information about you. If you message or comment on our pages, Meta's own privacy policy applies to those platforms.

Services we use

GitHub Pages hosts the site, Google Fonts provides the fonts, Web3Forms and our email provider deliver form messages, and Meta (Instagram, Facebook) hosts our social pages. Each handles data under its own policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order messages as long as needed to make, deliver and support your order, and new-arrival sign-ups until you ask us to stop.

Ask to see or delete your data

Email mayurighoshblg@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the name, email or phone number you used. We'll delete your messages and sign-up within 30 days and reply to confirm. You can ask what we hold about you the same way.
